Common questions

What should a flyer say?

The offer, who it is for, and how to act on it. A business name at the top and a list of services underneath is the commonest mistake - nobody is looking for you yet, so lead with what they get.

What size are flyers usually printed at?

A5 is standard for handouts and letterbox drops, A4 for noticeboards and shop windows. A5 is also cheaper in quantity, which matters at a thousand copies.

What paper should I use?

130-170gsm for handouts - light enough to carry a stack, heavy enough not to feel disposable. Anything glossier is only worth it if there are photographs, and there are none here.
