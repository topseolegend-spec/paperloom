Common questions

What size is a student ID card?

85 by 54mm - the same as a bank card, so it fits standard holders and laminating pouches. A portrait version for lanyards is in the editor.

Should the card carry a photograph?

Most schools attach one after printing rather than printing it in, which is what the space on these is for. It also means one printed batch works for a whole class.

What details should not go on a student card?

A home address, a date of birth, or a parent's phone number. A card that is lost should not tell a stranger where a child lives - the school's own number is the safe contact.
