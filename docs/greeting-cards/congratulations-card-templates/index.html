Common questions

What do you write in a congratulations card?

Name what they achieved and acknowledge what it cost. "Congratulations on the promotion - the last two years of it were not easy to watch, never mind live through" lands harder than praise alone.

Is a card still appropriate for a work achievement?

Yes, and it stands out precisely because most congratulations now arrive as messages. A physical card on a desk is read more than once.

Can I use one of these for an exam result?

Yes - change the greeting line to suit, and keep the message short. Results cards are usually opened in front of other people.
